# Security Fundamentals — Lesson Plan

Each lesson is one chapter of the [outline](outline.md). This course is concept-led: most lessons have little or no code, and lean on diagrams, tables, and small, concrete examples. A lesson that grows too big can split.

**Who it is for:** a complete beginner. No security, no programming, no networking. This is lesson 1 of the whole program.

## Lesson 1 Topics

The topics in [chapter 1 of the outline](outline.md#chapter-1-what-cybersecurity-is). Lesson 1 gives the whole picture at a shallow depth; later lessons return to the CIA triad, risk, controls, and ethics in depth. It has no code: the learner has not yet taken any programming course.
