## References

These are the main sources used to write this book, and good places to learn more. Security guidance and figures change over time, and laws differ by country, so check the current version of any source before relying on a specific detail. Web addresses were correct at the time of writing.

### Frameworks and standards

- **NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0** (2024) — the six functions used in section 10. https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework
- **NIST SP 800-30**, Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments — the risk ideas in section 3. https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/30/r1/final
- **NIST SP 800-63**, Digital Identity Guidelines — the password and authentication guidance in section 6. https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-3/
- **CIS Critical Security Controls v8.1** — the prioritized controls and Implementation Groups in section 10. https://www.cisecurity.org/controls
- **ISO/IEC 27001 and 27002** — the international standard and its control catalogue, section 10. https://www.iso.org/standard/27001
- **MITRE ATT&CK** — the knowledge base of attacker behavior, sections 5 and 10. https://attack.mitre.org

### Attacks, risks, and guidance

- **OWASP (Open Worldwide Application Security Project)** — the Top 10 and much more, sections 5 and 10. https://owasp.org
- **Verizon Data Breach Investigations Report (DBIR)** — evidence on how attacks actually start, sections 4 and 5. https://www.verizon.com/business/resources/reports/dbir/
- **CISA (US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency)** — practical defensive guidance and advisories. https://www.cisa.gov
- **Have I Been Pwned** — checking whether your accounts appear in known breaches, section 6. https://haveibeenpwned.com

### Law and ethics

- Computer-crime and data-protection law differs by country and changes. **Confirm the current law in Cambodia** before relying on any legal detail in section 9. The following are widely documented references for the *shape* of such law:
  - (US) Computer Fraud and Abuse Act — https://www.justice.gov/criminal/ccips
  - (EU) General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — https://gdpr.eu

### Careers

- **NIST NICE Workforce Framework for Cybersecurity** — the roles described in section 11. https://niccs.cisa.gov/workforce-development/nice-framework

*Every figure and legal statement in this book should be re-checked against these sources before publication, and dated.*
